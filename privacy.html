Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Ten Second Spike website and the Ten Second Spike Uploader, an internal tool used only by the owner of the Ten Second Spike YouTube channel.

What the uploader accesses

The uploader uses YouTube API Services, so YouTube and Google receive the data needed to upload and manage videos. When the channel owner signs in with Google, the uploader gets permission to manage that one YouTube channel. It uses this to upload videos, add titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, read the processing status of those videos, and change their visibility.

What we store

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. We do not store data about YouTube viewers, and the website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Third parties

Uploaded videos are hosted by YouTube. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access given to the uploader can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/connections. When access is removed, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the owner's computer.

Changes and contact
