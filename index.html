TEN SECOND SPIKE

The moments chat lost it over.

Ten Second Spike is a YouTube channel of short gaming clips. We watch live streams, count the chat every ten seconds, and clip the moments where chat suddenly explodes. Every clip credits the streamer it came from.

Ten Second Spike Uploader

The uploader is an internal tool that runs on our own computer. It uploads our clips to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and sets reviewed clips to public. Nobody else signs in to it.
